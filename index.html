    What makes a password strong?
    • Use at least 8 characters; longer is generally better.
    • Avoid names, birthdays and personal information.
    • Do not use sequences like 123456 or repeated digits.
    • Use unique, random combinations rather than predictable substitutions.
    • Use a different password for every account.
